About Zahid Rasheed

I help organizations build and adopt AI on sovereign infrastructure: systems where data, models, and inference stay within trusted boundaries. With growing geopolitical tension and cross-border data risk, I believe this will matter more every year for enterprises, public sector, and critical digital services.

Associate Vice President at Aeven A/S. Working with clients on AI enablement, platform modernization, and engineering leadership.

Previously at TDC Brands, where I grew from Senior Software Engineer to Head of Development, leading large engineering teams through business, organizational, and technical transformation and delivering measurable business outcomes across digital products used at national scale in TV, streaming, music, and connected home services.

Before that, Senior Software Engineer at Kayak (Booking Holdings), building consumer mobile products in travel.
